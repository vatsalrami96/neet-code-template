# Leadership Principles story bank

Amazon weighs these as much as the code. Every coding round embeds one or two LP questions, and the loop has
dedicated behavioral rounds. Build this over the five weeks, twenty minutes twice a week (Tuesday and Friday),
not in the last three days.

Format per story: STAR, but with numbers. Situation (one line), Task (one line), Action (three to five lines,
"I" not "we"), Result (a number or a concrete outcome), and one line on what you'd do differently.

Aim for two stories per principle for the eight below. Stories can serve more than one principle; note the mapping.
Interviewers probe with "what would you do differently" and "what was the hardest part", so keep those ready.
